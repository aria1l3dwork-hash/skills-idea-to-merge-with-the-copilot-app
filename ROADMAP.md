# Mona's Bookmark Roadmap

A practical next-step guide for making your bookmarks easier to organize, manage, and keep safe.

| Area | Item | Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Search & filter bookmarks | 🚀 | High |
| Management | Edit a saved bookmark | 🚀 | Medium |
| Organization | Tags or folders | 🚀 | Medium |
| Portability | Import & export bookmarks | 🚀 | Medium |
| Reliability | Duplicate slugs on re-add | 🐞 | High |
| Validation | Empty-URL validation | 🐞 | Medium |
| Data safety | Bookmarks lost when localStorage is cleared | 🐞 | Medium |

## 🚀 Planned features

- 🔖 Search & filter bookmarks — priority: high
- ✏️ Edit a saved bookmark — priority: medium
- 🗂️ Organize bookmarks with tags or folders — priority: medium
- 📦 Import & export bookmarks — priority: medium

## 🐞 Known bugs & risks

- 🐞 Duplicate slugs on re-add — priority: high
- 🐞 Empty-URL validation — priority: medium
- 🐞 Bookmarks are lost when localStorage is cleared — priority: medium
